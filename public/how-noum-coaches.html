How Noum coaches

Only what the evidence can support.

Noum helps you practise communication under pressure. Its feedback should become more specific only when repeated evidence earns that confidence.

Your words are the receipt

When Noum cites a strong moment, it uses a verified line from a completed rep. It does not invent a quote or treat a generated rewrite as something you said.

Early reads stay tentative

One rep can suggest a useful next exercise. Repeated patterns can support a stronger intervention. Missing or mixed evidence should remain visible instead of being turned into certainty.

One lever, then review

Noum builds around one current communication lever, prescribes a focused repeat, compares later evidence, and adapts when the plan is not holding.

Practice is not a real-world outcome

Improvement inside Noum does not prove that a meeting, interview, or presentation improved. Real-world check-ins are kept separate so the coach can say what remains unproven.

Your communication content stays under your control

Noum provides cloud-processing choices, account export and deletion controls, and a public privacy policy. Growth and commercial measurement must not contain prompts, transcripts, recordings, goals, names, or generated coaching text.

What Noum cannot infer

Noum cannot read motives, diagnose a personality, guarantee professional outcomes, or replace a qualified human coach. It should not claim otherwise.
