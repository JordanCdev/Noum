# App Store screenshot and preview brief

Use a signed release-candidate build with a deidentified, source-bound fixture.
Every visible metric and quote must be generated by that fixture. Do not invent
testimonials, ratings, outcomes, or coach credentials.

The final screenshot set uses the 6.9-inch portrait target at 1320 × 2868 with
no alpha channel. The final preview uses the accepted iPhone portrait target at
886 × 1920, no more than 30 fps, and H.264 or ProRes 422 HQ. Capture the assets
only after the exact signed TestFlight candidate, source commit, version/build,
and deidentified fixture are frozen. The checked-in visual QA screenshots are
not App Store marketing assets.

## Seven-shot default sequence

1. **Communicate clearly under pressure** — Train recommendation with one plan-built rep.
2. **Complete a focused spoken rep** — recording state with calm timer and target.
3. **See evidence from your own words** — Summary with verified quote and restrained read.
4. **Follow one next move** — prescribed retry with exact focus and demand.
5. **Watch a pattern become evidence** — comparison or weekly read that names uncertainty.
6. **Prepare for the real moment** — upcoming interview, presentation, or stakeholder meeting.
7. **Your words stay under your control** — How Noum coaches / privacy and memory controls.

## Preview storyboard, 20–30 seconds

- 0–4s: choose the recommended rep.
- 4–12s: speak one short answer; no staged waveform unrelated to audio.
- 12–19s: summary lands with one verified quote and one observation.
- 19–25s: tap the prescribed next rep.
- 25–30s: first-week read and evidence-boundary end card.

Motion must respect the app's restrained visual language. Avoid rapid cuts,
confetti, full-screen achievement cinematics, or claims of guaranteed change.

Each CPP and PPO treatment gets a dedicated first screenshot; shots 2–7 reuse
the evidence sequence so the experiment changes one message at a time. The
authoritative mapping is `release-assets.template.json`. Do not rename or
reorder assets ad hoc in App Store Connect.
