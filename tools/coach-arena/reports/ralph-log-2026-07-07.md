## Change 1 — tighten coach turn instructions

Files changed:
- `Noum/CoachPromptBundle.swift`

Why changed:
- The worst fixtures were not mainly missing facts. They were failing because replies sounded report-like, overlong, generic, or did not adapt to the user's exact objection.

Failures addressed:
- `what-do-you-know` metadata/spec-sheet voice.
- `okay-thats-cool-however` partial pushback.
- `its-not-easy` and `exhausted` emotional fatigue.
- `goal-change-engaging` and `set-authoritative` confirmation-card handling.
- `talk-went-well`, `interview-prep`, `leadership-update`, and `big-moment` transfer/prep specificity.
- Scaffold/report residue such as `Next rep:`, `Filler rate:`, `Read:`, `Move:`, and `Target:`.

Expected improvement:
- Higher dialogue-feel and EQ scores on low fixtures.
- Fewer too-long flags.
- Better partial-pushback handling by adapting the technique instead of repeating it.
- Better metadata-trap handling by answering in person-shaped language.

Possible regression risk:
- Shorter budgets may under-serve genuinely complex plan requests.
- Extra universal instructions increase prompt length slightly.
- Provider may obey brevity too strongly on deep assessment unless the plan/deep-assessment exceptions fire.
