# Coach hardening transcript notes

All examples below are synthetic. No user transcript, prompt, email, account
identifier, or coach response was placed in the exported support bundle.

## Goal-change contract

**User:** I think I want to sound more engaging.

**Expected safe coach move:** Treat “engaging” as a proposed training emphasis,
not a new identity. Map it to the nearest supported emphases (Storytelling for
shape or Warm for connection), retain the already-observed Authoritative work,
and ask one practical question about the outcome the user wants. Do not say the
goal was changed, ask the user to tap/confirm, or imply their prior delivery was
fake or inauthentic.

The bounded context test confirms that prior evidence is summarized only as the
current lever plus evidence depth/age; raw transcript, metric, and case-basis
details are not dumped into the reply context.

## Breakdown → repair → adapt

**User:** The pause thing made me lose my thought completely.

**Follow-up:** I tried again. Still awkward—once it goes quiet I start rebuilding
the whole answer in my head. What do I do instead?

**Expected repair:** Acknowledge that the silent-pause mechanism failed, preserve
the point-first goal, and replace the mechanism: repeat one keyword aloud, then
finish one sentence from it. Do not prescribe another silent pause, “slow down,”
or blame the user for applying the advice badly.

## 3,000-character pipeline case

turn; the observability support bundle receives only canonical stage/outcome

## Privacy regression

The support-bundle test injects a synthetic secret token and email address into
provider/event reason fields. Neither value appears in the exported bundle.
Reasons are reduced to canonical values such as `stage:coach.accepted` and
`provider-outcome:accepted`; raw in-app diagnostics remain local.

## Readiness statement

Local behavior and contracts are covered, but this is not external production
evidence. The offline replay run covered all 63 cases, with a 69.6 mean against
a 70 target plus legacy fixture-floor and placeholder/intent failures.
Live-provider, physical-device, real-microphone, lifecycle, professional
calibration, and durable real-user outcome gates therefore remain **NO-GO**.
